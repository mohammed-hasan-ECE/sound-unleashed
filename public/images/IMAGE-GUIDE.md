# Image Asset Guide

Place product images in the following locations:

## Product Images

Each product should have a corresponding image file named according to its slug:

1. `yamaha-c40-classical-guitar.jpg` or `.png` - Yamaha C40 Classical Guitar
2. `yamaha-p145-digital-piano.jpg` or `.png` - Yamaha P-145 Digital Piano
3. `yamaha-psr-e383-keyboard.jpg` or `.png` - Yamaha PSR-E383 Portable Keyboard
4. `alesis-nitro-max-drum-kit.jpg` or `.png` - Alesis Nitro Max Electronic Drum Kit
5. `yamaha-f310-acoustic-guitar.jpg` or `.png` - Yamaha F310 Natural Acoustic Guitar
6. `fender-fa125-acoustic-guitar.jpg` or `.png` - Fender FA-125 Dreadnought Acoustic Guitar
7. `yamaha-c40m-classical-guitar.jpg` or `.png` - Yamaha C40M Classical Guitar
8. `casio-ct-s1-keyboard.jpg` or `.png` - Casio CT-S1 Keyboard
9. `roland-juno-di-synthesizer.jpg` or `.png` - Roland JUNO-Di Synthesizer
10. `akai-mpk-mini-play-mk3.jpg` or `.png` - Akai MPK Mini Play MK3 Controller Keyboard

## Image Specifications

- Aspect ratio: 1:1 (square) for product cards
- Minimum size: 300x300px
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Background: transparent or white for consistent card display

## Fallback Behavior

When images are not available, a neutral placeholder will be shown. Do not use placeholder images as final product photos.
